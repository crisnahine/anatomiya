## Decision

A source root answers a new test, in `check` and the notice, only where the family it counts is the test's own

## Why

A `_spec.rb` under eight `.js` files was told `0 of 8 .js files have a namesake test`, a count about files no Ruby test could cover.
Asked of every tracked test of 60 repositories as if just added, 46,820 paths, the build before the hold gave 5 findings and the hold gave the same 5 (`docs/measurements/2026-10-11-test-by-address.md`): the cross-family answer is reached only by the contrived shape, and the hold removes it without moving any real finding.
It applies to every family, since one condition reads simpler than a branch and the tree-sitter findings did not move
