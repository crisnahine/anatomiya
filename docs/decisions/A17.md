# A17

Section: A. Delivery

## Decision

Nothing is built to put a map back into a running session. A delivery lasts one context window, and the platform already rebuilds it

## Why

Measured over 12,500 transcripts, `docs/measurements/2026-08-17-context-delivery.md`: 912 deliveries in 334 sessions, 84 paths delivered more than once, 46 of those with a compaction in between.
Of the twelve sessions that compacted after taking a delivery, nine took a path back; on this tool's own files all six repeats followed a compaction.
So the dedup is against the window, not a per-session latch, and the two halves come back differently: the overview carries no `paths` key and is re-injected from disk at the boundary, an area file returns on the next read that matches it.
The platform documents both under [what survives compaction](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/context-window#what-survives-compaction), and its `InstructionsLoaded` hook carries `compact` as a load reason.
Issue #44 read the same once-per-area delivery and concluded compaction was terminal, from a session that never compacted: none of the five transcripts holding a delivery on that repository has a boundary in it.
A `SessionStart` hook on `compact` would therefore duplicate the platform and spend the window the compaction had just freed, which is the cost the issue itself named.
What stands is the stretch between two rebuilds, where the counts sit at their delivery turn and the work moves away from them; `SessionStart` fires at boundaries and cannot reach it

## Status

**done** as a decision not to build; `scripts/measure-delivery.mjs` is what re-measures it.
The measurement is the deliverable: A6, A7 and A8 all rest on how long a delivery lasts and none of them had a count behind the word "session"
