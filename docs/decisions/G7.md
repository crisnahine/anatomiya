## Decision

The excluded directories cover the other names deliberately unidiomatic code goes by

## Why

Measured: angular keeps 2,010 files of golden compiler output under `test_cases`, which was 55 of its 339 areas and every one of its 476 unparseable files.
`test_cases`, `testdata`, `test-data`, `golden`, `goldens`, `__mocks__` and `mocks` are 2,218 collected files across 35 repositories, and `.yarn` is 14 more, every one a machine-generated bundle whose 2 MB releases sit at the parse timeout boundary and flip between crashed and rejected across runs, which moves the always-loaded overview (A5).
`examples` is deliberately left in: 8,967 paths match it and much of that is maintained code
