Section: A. Delivery

## Decision

The files directly at the repository root are an area where their language declares the root a package (`rootPackage`, which Go alone states) and they clear the floor counted alone. The area owns those files and none below, nothing folds into it, and it prints as `the repository root`

## Why

A Go library keeps its package at the module root, and with the root never an area that code answered no claim: gin holds 40 of its 98 source files there and caddy 39 of 371.
Scanned with the root left out and with it an area: gin 3 areas and 4, 42 files in no area and 2, 1 of 3 claims stated and 1 of 4; caddy 28 and 29, 41 and 2, 5 of 28 and 6 of 29; hugo 62 and 63, 57 and 52, 0 of 102 and 0 of 103, its root holding 5 files at a floor of 5. gin's layout line still ends `and 53 at the repository root`, since that clause counts where files sit (H32).
Claude Code reads `paths` by gitignore's rules, where a pattern holding no slash matches at every depth: on the three file lists `*.go` reaches 99, 371 and 914 files, and `/*.go` the 40, 39 and 5 at the root
