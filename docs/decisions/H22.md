# H22

Section: H. Layout

## Decision

A file named by Component Story Format's own convention, `*.stories.<ext>`, is its own kind, present above zero as `record.stories`, and is excluded from the producer and sibling-module populations rather than sorted into either by its incidental syntax. The two populations are one definition, `isProducer` (H54)

## Why

A story is neither source to imitate nor a test: storybook's `.stories.tsx` carries literal JSX and joined the ordinary component population, while its `.stories.ts` carried none and joined `helperFacet`'s sibling modules, so the always-loaded overview's own top-three stem list read `2559 sibling modules named index/types/input.stories`, filing that repository's most common fixture name beside private helper functions.
About a fifth of its component-shaped population, never disclosed as what it is.
`isStoryFile` matches the filename alone, since the two syntactic flavours are one convention and the reader's question is the same for both.
The count is absent rather than zero, the way `companions` and `helpers` already are, since most repositories hold none
