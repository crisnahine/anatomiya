# A119

Section: A. Delivery

## Decision

Whether the echo delivers on a call is decided in its own module, `echo.mjs`: `echoDue` answers from the map's digest, the window's transcript and the plugin's data directory, and `heldIn` is the walk of the window. The delivery measurement replays kept transcripts through `heldIn`, the walk the hook runs

## Why

The replay in `scripts/measure-echo.mjs` carried a forward copy of the rule that counted the held delivery's own bytes toward the window and read back past 1 MiB, so its count of what the rule keeps was not the hook's.
On 3,848 local transcripts holding 32,591 deliveries on 2026-10-12, the hook's walk keeps 8,063 (24.7%) where that copy kept 8,581 (26.3%).
The decision sat inside `echoContext`, behind the map on disk, so each of its 31 tests scanned a Ruby fixture to get a map, and a machine without prism skipped every one.
Taken from a digest, a transcript and a directory, the decision is tested with no map: the 32 tests of `test/echo.test.mjs` ran with no `ruby` on PATH on 2026-10-12.
The replay through the walk takes longer: 87 s on that store against 51 s for the forward copy.
Given up in the replay: a transcript line that carries a delivery the rule drops beside a text it keeps stays whole, held copies included, and the replay counts a call's own `tool_result` where the hook ran before it was written, which is reasoned from the order of the records and was not measured
