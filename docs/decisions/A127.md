# A127

Section: A. Delivery

## Decision

The overview head, the check's command file and the reuse check's reason each tell the model two things in its own words: report work as it happened, never a test, a read or a check that did not run and never a site changed to get past a rule, fix it or say why it was left; and where the map or the plugin is wrong about the code, tell the user and ask whether to file it at github.com/crisnahine/anatomiya/issues

## Why

A model told to follow a convention can satisfy the count by moving the construct the row counts, or report a check it never ran; the map gave it the tools and the permission to say unverified (A16) and said nothing about reporting what it did.
A defect in the plugin, a predicate that misread a construct or a claim the map should not have stated, is seen first by the model reading the wrong claim, and it had nowhere to send one.
The overview head is the one surface every session reads, so both sit there as the fourth constant line, one line and not two, since two lines of head cost the areas of an overview at its bound their listing (the roster fixture of `test/render.test.mjs` lost both of its areas to two); the check's command file repeats them where a finding tempts the dodge, and the reuse check's reason where a search can be reported and not run.
The reuse reason's measured wording (A123, A124) is unchanged up to the new sentence, which follows it; the 24 of 24 and the A/B numbers describe the wording before it, and a rerun of those is the measurement this change still owes.
