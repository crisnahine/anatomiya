# F27

Section: F. Safety

## Decision

The co-change walk is bounded: `--max-count=20000` streamed through the git runner's clock and field cap; a change stops being stored at its 41st added or modified file or its 10,001st distinct deleted file name; pairs are counted only for a class with 35 candidates and a companion touched 32 times, at most 100,000 pair keys, past which the count stops and states nothing, and the scan's summary says so; the record keeps at most 100 stated claims and 100 near misses, and every reader reads at most 100 stated of the first 200; `GIT_NO_LAZY_FETCH` with nothing that needs a blob; and a git that refuses `--diff-merges` states nothing, and the summary says so too. A failed scan stops the walk's git

## Why

The repository is untrusted, and its history is as long as it chooses.
Measured with the build: at most 7,618 pair keys counted (alphagov/whitehall), against 593,687 unpruned (next.js); the walk and the tip's tree 46.3 s over the 44 repositories and 3.95 s at most (opf/openproject), at a load of 7 to 85; 7.5 MB of log for vscode's 20,000 changes (`docs/measurements/2026-10-11-cochange.md`).
The walk starts once the pin answers, beside the parse, and a scan that fails after it started aborts its signal so no `git log` outlives the scan
