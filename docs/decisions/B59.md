# B59

Section: B. Engine

## Decision

A Kotlin file the vendored grammar cannot read stays unread and is named as a file this tool's grammar could not read. No row counts it, and the grammar is not traded for a fork that reads more

## Why

Unread on the three measured repositories: okhttp 7 of 617 files and 7.88% of lines, ktor 82 of 2,596 and 6.79%, kotlinx.serialization 5 of 720 and 1.32%.
Each of the 94 was read at the node the grammar reports and through the file, since in 3 of 10 picked at random the reported line holds nothing unusual and the cause is elsewhere in the file.
None is a syntax error: the Kotlin 2.4.21 compiler's parser, run in a container, accepts all 94 (`docs/measurements/2026-10-08-reference-parsers.md`).
Classed by the rewrite that lets the grammar read each file: 27 need a named context parameter (`context(scope: Scope)`) taken out, 16 a `get` or `set` that opens the line after a local declaration, 11 a `$$"` string, 10 a primary constructor on the line after the class header, 6 `dynamic` as a type or a name, 5 an annotation before an accessor, 5 a `when` guard, 5 a context clause in a function type, and 12 one of eight rarer forms (`open(` as a call, a local named `in1`, a raw string closing on `""""`, `suspend { }`, `enum` as a name); 3 files need two, so the classes sum to 97.
`@willbooster/tree-sitter-kotlin` 3.0.0 read 71 of the 94 and was refused: a fork published the day before it was measured, 35 releases in ten days, and its wasm would run in a parser child on other people's repositories.
For scale, on their three measured repositories the other six languages leave at most 0.97% of a repository's lines unread, serilog apart at 4.35% for one C# file; next.js, outside them, leaves 9.71% of its Rust lines unread (B63), and `tree-sitter-rust` v0.24.2, which reads 5 of the 23 files rustc accepts and the grammar leaves unread (`raw` as a name after `&`, `unsafe extern` in a function), is a GitHub release that never reached npm, so it is not taken: every vendored grammar is held to its npm package by hash, and a GitHub-only wasm needs a vendoring route and a trust check of its own (`docs/measurements/2026-10-11-rust-unread.md`)

## Status

**done** `plugins/anatomiya/grammars/kotlin.wasm`, `langs.mjs` (`grammarFor`), `tree-sitter-file.mjs` (`parseTreeFile`).
What this gives up: 1.32% to 7.88% of the lines of the three Kotlin repositories answer no row and no facet, and Kotlin's row is counted over the rest.
Context parameters and `$$` strings are 3.05% of ktor's lines and none of okhttp's, where two test files with a local named `in1` or `in2` hold 8,804 of its 11,235 unread lines
Upstream, tree-sitter-kotlin's scanner emits no member terminator before a `}` on the same line: issues #10 and #12 at https://github.com/tree-sitter-grammars/tree-sitter-kotlin are open, with fixes in pull requests #11 and #13, unreleased as of 1.1.0; a grammar release that merges them is the way those files become read.
