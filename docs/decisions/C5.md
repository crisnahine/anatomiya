# C5

Section: C. The counted-claim model

## Decision

Dimensions carry `precision: precise | partial`. A `partial` dimension can never reach top severity

## Why

3 of 8 measured predicates are partial

## Status

**done** `dimensions.mjs` (`assertPrecision`), `check.mjs` (`severityFor`), `pairing.mjs`, `render.mjs` (`partialNote`, which prints the partial warning on counts lines too, the matches-model-default and the no-convention form alike).
The cap was enforced; carrying the field was not.
`severityFor` reads `precision !== "precise"`, so a row that forgets it or spells it `"Precise"` is capped by the same comparison a deliberate `partial` is, silently, and reads in the map as a claim nobody marked.
Checked at load rather than in a test, because the registry is assembled from six files and the obligations were the sixth: they are not in `ALL_DIMENSIONS`, and every per-file precision test had missed them
