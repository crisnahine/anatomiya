# H4

Section: H. Layout

## Decision

Root selection scales with the corpus: a floor of `max(3, ceil(0.01 * N))` cumulative files, descent through any directory one child holds 80% of, and at most 7 lines. The three numbers are tuned by measurement; that they scale is the decision

## Why

A fixed floor right for a 200-file repository makes every directory of a 100,000-file one a root, which is the failure area discovery already answered with a scaled floor.
80% is what separates a wrapper from a parent.
7 lines is what the section can spend without crowding the listing it sits above, and the whole section is 15 lines of a 40-line file.
A directory under the floor folds into the nearest root above it or into `and N more directories holding M files`, and the direct files of a descended directory are their own candidate, because rubocop prints `lib/rubocop (files at this level): 45 .rb` beside `lib/rubocop/cop`.
A descent that earns no line at all keeps the directory instead, because webpack's `lib` is 652 files with 117 at that level and no child clearing its 144-file floor, and the map named `test` and `examples` and never webpack's source
