# A51

Section: A. Delivery

## Decision

The binary's verbs are one table, `COMMANDS`, and each verb carries its own arm as `run`; the dispatch is one call into it, and a guard reads the table and refuses a verb with no arm

## Why

The verbs were listed three times: the table, a dispatch chain of six named arms ending in a bare `else` that scanned, and the usage text.
A verb added to the table with no arm silently scanned.
The usage stays a hand-written subset (A24); the table is the one reachable owner
