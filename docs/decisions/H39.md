## Decision

The map states, in a sentence, that a directory with no test precedent outranks an instruction to always write a test

## Why

The map states measurements and a testing skill states an Iron Law.
Read side by side the law wins, because one is phrased as data and the other as an instruction, and the session that prompted this was under both and resolved the conflict silently in favour of the skill.
A principle rather than a second finding: it is about how to read the counts, not about a file.
It appears only where such a directory is on the page.
The gate has two halves.
The first is the one H38 uses, some root pairing three or more of its files with namesake tests, so a repository that pairs no tests anywhere is never told it has departed from something it never had; it is asked of every counted root, since it is a fact about the repository and needs no line.
The second, a root with producers and fewer than three namesake tests, is asked of the roots the overview prints, because it is the directory the sentence is about and a reader has to find its line.
Measured on 56 scanned repositories, the 35 older and the 21 of H48's seven languages, read off each record and overview: the record stores the sentence's key where some counted root arms it, and the overview prints the sentence where a printed root does.
3 of the 35 older ones store the key armed only by roots the budget folds away, backstage by `docs-ui/src/app/components` (0 of 72), prisma by `examples` (2 of 228) and next.js by `turbopack/crates` (0 of 6), `packages/next/src/compiled` (0 of 682) and `packages/next-codemod/transforms/__testfixtures__` (0 of 238), and none of the three prints the sentence.
Of the 21 newer, jellyfin is the same, by `src/Jellyfin.Database` (0 of 261).
No repository prints it without the key, 29 store it and print it and 23 do neither, flask among those: its `examples` reads 4 of 10 by H50's Python rules and arms nothing. vscode stores no key: the one root it held with no paired file is `src/vscode-dts`, 177 declaration files, which sit in no count (H21). fastlane prints `screengrab` (1 of 19 `.java`, under five printed Ruby roots at 15 to 86 namesakes) and the sentence with it.
Against release 0.13.4 the sentence is gone from four of the older overviews: prisma and next.js, where that release prints it with the arming root folded; backstage, where that release prints the arming root (`docs-ui/src/app/components`) and this build folds it; and vscode, where that release counts `src/vscode-dts` at 0 of 145. fastlane gains it, since that release reads no Java and counts nothing under `screengrab`, and Homebrew gains it for `docs`, which that release prints with no count and this build counts at 0 of 3 `.rb` (H21). babel and junit5 print it on roots the page shows: babel prints four roots at 0 or 1 namesakes of 53 to 95 files and folds `packages/babel-traverse`, its one root at 3, and junit5 prints `documentation` at 0 of 14 and folds its four paired roots.
With both halves asked of the printed roots those two print none, over directories the page shows.
A sentence the printed roots do not arm holds no line of the budget: the roots are fitted without it, up to the first that would arm it.
On backstage, prisma, next.js and jellyfin the next root in line is the one that arms the sentence (`docs-ui/src/app/components`, `examples`, `turbopack/crates`, `src/Jellyfin.Database`), the two cannot both have the line, and the section leaves it.
All four overviews are 40 lines, and on three the Areas listing names one area: backstage's `docs-ui/src/app/components` (129 files, 4 stated), the directory that arms the sentence, next.js's `.github/actions/next-stats-action/src` (16 files, 1 stated) and jellyfin's `Jellyfin.Api/Controllers` (60 files, 1 stated).
Where the budget is one line short of holding every stored sentence (4 lines and one per sentence), the unarmed sentence is the one left out: the other sentences print and no root line does, which is the page a record without the key prints.
With every stored sentence armed, that budget drops all the sentences and prints a root.
That budget is 6 or 7 lines with the keys a scan stores.
Given up: a folded directory with no precedent goes unspoken in the overview, and H38's notice still answers a test written into one.
A root the page has room for can fold with it: where the next root in line arms the sentence and one line is left for the two, that root folds and every root after it folds too, since roots print in order.
With six roots, the fifth at 0 of 10, a budget of 12 lines prints 11, four roots and the fold line, where the fifth root alone would fit; at 13 all six print with the sentence.
Separate from H38 because either could ship without the other: one answers after the branch is written and one is read before it is
