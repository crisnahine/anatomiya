# D9

Section: D. Gates

## Decision

The side a **suppressed** slot prints needs a majority the sample can support, not a plurality: the counter's own Wilson lower bound over a half, and a side holding no site at all never prints

## Why

`handler_is_named` is 799 of 1,054 = 0.758 repo-wide, and in one 7-file area it is 3 of 7 named, so the plurality flipped and that area printed "an event handler prop is given an inline arrow, not a named function" while other areas of the same repository printed the forward claim.
One dimension, one run, two directly opposed sentences.
Worse than reported: `check` then reported a file doing the thing 76% of the repository does as a violation of the inverse, reproduced end to end.
The guess that D8 would close it is wrong, and the reason is worth keeping: the slot fails `ratio` on both sides, which is checked before `evidence`, so the pooled prior never runs, and the sentence is chosen in `statedSide`, which reads raw counts and has no gate input at all.
The bar is algebraically "the counter's lower bound beats the claim's upper bound", since the Wilson interval is symmetric.
Measured against the case C6 exists for: 61 sites at 59 to 2 still flip, 10 at 9 to 1 flip, 10 at 8 to 2 do not, and 7 at 4 to 3 do not.
The one behaviour lost is a 5-of-9 area that used to print the counter and now prints the claim with 4 of 9, which is a directory with no habit saying so.
Amended after the fact, and the amendment carries more weight than the rule: the bound is penalised hardest at small n, so a unanimous counter of three failed it too and the map printed the claim with 0 of 3, a sentence the directory follows zero times, which `check` then reported against files doing exactly what every other file there does.
Measured on the same 2,486-file front end: of 441 suppressed two-sided rows, 81 printed a side with no site behind it, every one of them unanimous, and one parent and its own child printed directly opposed sentences off the same run, `src/components/forms` at 15 of 15 through `React.` and `src/components/forms/utils` at 0 of 2 by the bare name.
That is the defect D9 exists to close, recreated at small n.
One site each way is still mixed and the plurality rule still decides it, and so is one site against none: without a floor a single site flipped a directory against a claim the rest of the repository holds at 0.947 and against its own parent area's directive, which is this row's own defect at n = 1.
Of the 81 unanimous counters on that repository 29 are a single site, and the count printed beside the sentence is what says how thin the other 52 are
