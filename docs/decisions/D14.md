Section: D. Gates

## Decision

A co-change claim passes D1's ratio and Wilson bound with no borrowing, the D4 author bar over its conforming candidates, and three gates of its own: the companion is touched at twice its base rate or more, the companion and a producer exist at the tip, and the newest fifth of the candidates, at least 5, conforms at 0.90. A parent class passes only where its candidates came from at least two child directories and at least half of them from child directories that still hold a producer at the tip. One statement per pair: the directory's own class over its parent unless the parent covers a tenth more candidates, and the larger side where both directions clear

## Why

Measured at 4,000 changes on 44 repositories (`docs/measurements/2026-10-11-cochange.md`): the probe's gates held 445 of 491 held-out candidates (90.6%); adding the test, existence and recency gates, 276 of 285 (96.8%); with Wilson, 231 of 236 (97.9%).
Mined over all 4,000 changes, the Wilson bound stops 197 near misses, where the author, lift, existence and recency gates after it stop 3, and keeping it is what lets the check grade a perfect claim MUST-FIX on the bound every other claim faces.
Recency stops 2 near misses on cal.diy there.
At 20,000 the existence gate stops rubocop's `lib/rubocop/cop/rails/*.rb`, 51 of 51 with `CHANGELOG.md`, and eslint's `docs/rules/*.md`, 99 of 100 with a new `lib/rules/*.js`, both directories gone at the tip.
One child's habit, gone at the tip, is no evidence for a sibling the tree still holds: forem's `db/*/*.rb` and `vendor/*/*.gem`, each from one child, stop at the existence gate.
One statement per pair held opf/openproject to one claim where the probe stated a class and its parent over the same changes
