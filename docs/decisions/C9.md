Section: C. The counted-claim model

## Decision

An obligation learns its companion root from the corpus, with the declared pair as the prior

## Why

Measured: alphagov/whitehall keeps model tests under `test/unit/app/models` and scored 0 of 160 with 117 namesakes one prefix away, now 117 of 160; discourse answers its controllers in `spec/requests`, which no hardcoded pair reaches.
Learned on the path tail rather than the basename, because basename matching was ambiguous for 388 of one repository's services.
A tie learns nothing and the declared pair stands, or an obligation is decided by a filename
