# C29

Section: C. The counted-claim model

## Decision

Of eight proposed rows one ships, and every refusal is recorded with the measurement behind it

## Why

The proposal's own "clears 0.90" column reads a point ratio while the gate reads a Wilson lower bound, which is wrong for four of the seven, and its counts are repository-wide while the gates run per area.
Measured across seven repositories each: `hook_per_module` spreads 0.7778 react, 0.8235 storybook, 0.9224 supabase, 0.9259 next.js, 0.9367 cal.diy, 0.9429 backstage, 0.9800 empire-flippers/client, a 0.2022 spread with two repositories under the gate, so it ships.
The first-ranked proposal does not: expectations naming their argument rather than `anything` reads 0.9003 to 0.9883 across seven Ruby repositories, a spread of 0.088 with nothing under the gate, which is the `module_state_const` shape CONTRIBUTING's bar was written for.
A comment carrying no ticket reference is 0.999 on the one repository measured, same refusal.
A mutation's `onError` at 0.8850 and a date wrapper at 0.9033 both fail the bound, and the second cannot clear it at any realistic area size.
An `eslint-disable` naming a live rule needs a repository-config channel no row can reach and is the linter's own job.
The data-fetch routing row is left planned rather than dropped: 92 of 96 is a bound of 0.8977, three conforming sites short, and it has not been measured per area.
The eighth is planned for a different reason and is the one worth coming back to: `schema_matches_migrations`, that `db/schema.rb` changes only what this branch's own migrations produce, is a branch invariant rather than a ratio over sites, so it needs a fourth row kind beside tree, corpus and pairing, and a reader in the check.
Three of its four measured hits sit on one pull request whose head would have shipped a schema dropping two columns that exist in production
