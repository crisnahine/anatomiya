# H42

Section: H. Layout

## Decision

Every order a reader sees or a file records is by UTF-16 code unit, through one `byCode` in `paths.mjs`: the ties that pick a winner, and the whole lists too, which are the area list in `areas.mjs`, the pin and its delta in `baseline.mjs`, the finding order in `check.mjs` and the file list in `revision.mjs`

## Why

`localeCompare` orders by the host's locale and ICU tables, so two machines rendered two maps from one repository.
`layout.mjs`, `siblings.mjs` and `scripts/measure-layout.mjs` each carried the comparator privately, `companions.mjs` spelled it inline on the root that gets rendered, and `precedent.mjs` still broke the tie between two untested roots by locale, and the winner's `dir` lands in a finding's area.
The whole lists were first left on `localeCompare`, on the reading that they order every member rather than pick one.
That was wrong for the area list: the overview names areas in its order until the line budget runs out, so the order picks the names.
Measured on 50 stated areas named `a00` to `a24` and `ä00` to `ä24`, `LC_ALL=en_US.UTF-8` named 8 of the `ä` areas and `sv_SE.UTF-8` named none.
The pin was written, and its delta printed, in the host's order as well
