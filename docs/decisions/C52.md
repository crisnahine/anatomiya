# C52

Section: C. The counted-claim model

## Decision

No row asks whether an import names what it takes, in any of the seven languages. Kotlin's was measured and left to the linter that already refuses a wildcard; Python, Java and Rust were measured and did not clear the bar; PHP, C# and Go were not asked

## Why

ktlint is Kotlin's standard linter, `no-wildcard-imports` is in its standard rule set, and its documentation says every rule of that set is enabled by default.
Over a tree-sitter tree the question read 1.0000, 0.3649, 0.2705 (okhttp 6,260 of 6,260, ktor 4,747 of 13,008, kotlinx.serialization 693 of 2,562), spread 0.7295, and a scan stated it in 33 of okhttp's 57 slots, in none of ktor's 157 or kotlinx.serialization's 56, and in 4 of junit5's 9. okhttp and junit5 run ktlint through spotless with no rule switched off, in the projects that apply it, ktor's `.editorconfig` sets `ktlint_standard_no-wildcard-imports = disabled`, and kotlinx.serialization configures no ktlint.
By the build configuration, read with no Gradle run, 31 of the 37 stated areas sit in a project that applies it: 30 of okhttp's and junit5's `documentation/src/test/kotlin/example/kotlin`.
Six do not: okhttp's `android-test` and `android-test-app` (3 areas, 363 imports) apply `okhttp.base-conventions` without `okhttp.quality-conventions`, and junit5's included build under `gradle/plugins` (3 areas, 276 imports) applies `kotlin-dsl` and no ktlint.
So 31 of the 37 stated lines restated a rule the build already fails on, and where the rule is off the row printed counts: the fifth test of the bar in CONTRIBUTING.md refuses it. ktlint's FAQ states one exception: under its `intellij_idea` and `android_studio` code styles the rule allows `java.util.*` unless `ij_kotlin_packages_to_use_import_on_demand` is set.
The other languages measured: Python 0.9970, 1.0000, 1.0000 (django, flask, fastapi) and Java 1.0000, 0.9999, 1.0000 (commons-lang, junit5, gson), spreads of 0.0030 and 0.0001, a default; Rust 0.8807, 0.9832, 0.9130 (ripgrep, tokio, serde), one repository under 0.90 and a spread of 0.1025 against 0.15.
PHP, C# and Go were not measured.
A PHP `use` names one class, function or constant, or a braced group of named ones, and a C# `using` names a namespace, so neither language has a wildcard form to count.
Go's nearest form is the dot import, which is 1 of 9,863 import lines in caddy, gin and hugo (0 of 3,070, 0 of 541 and 1 of 6,252), too rare to state

## Status

**done** nothing shipped: no row, test, witness pair, model default or counter pin holds the key `wildcard_import`, and `docs/dimension-intake.md` keeps the entry as dropped.
No row in the tree counts these sites, so `docs/measurements/2026-10-07-refused-rows.md` is the record of these ratios and says which of the numbers here it holds no output for.
