# H30

Section: H. Layout

## Decision

The tests line says how many of a runner's files are under the directory it names, and the namesake clause names the directory its denominator was counted over

## Why

Two readings of one line, both false and both in the file that loads every turn.
`majorityDir` names the directory four fifths of a runner's files sit in, deliberately, because a strict prefix collapses on the one file 28 of 35 repositories keep outside the tree; the renderer then printed the whole count beside that name, so rails-api read `1333 RSpec specs under spec` two lines under its own `- spec: 1332 RSpec specs`, and a reader could only take them for one claim.
It now reads `1332 of 1333`, and only where the two differ.
The second clause took its denominator from the largest source root and printed it bare, so `1046 of 1575 .rb files have a namesake test` read repository-wide when 1575 was `app/services` alone.
Both halves are wording rather than arithmetic: the counts either side of them were already right, and the namesake figure moves from 1046 to 1045 only because H29 stops counting a commented-out spec.
The third complaint against this line, that the folded-directory count reconciles with nothing, does not survive the arithmetic: the roster counts every tracked file, 8,986 of them, the seven named roots hold 5,426, and the difference is exactly 3,560

## Status

**done** `layout.mjs` (`testsLine` carries `under`, and the record carries `dir` beside `path` so the clause names a directory rather than the `(files at this level)` label), `render-layout.mjs` (`namesakeClause` takes the population's root, and the tests line prints the pair), pinned by `test/render.test.mjs`; `scripts/measure-layout.mjs` recounts both clauses.
Revised for a root that holds one level only: its count is the files directly in `lib`, and `4 of 4 .js files under lib` read as the whole subtree two lines below `lib/sub: 0 of 4`.
The clause names such a root by its label, `under lib (files at this level)`, which is the population it counted; every other root's label is its directory, so nothing else moves
