# B14

Section: B. Engine

## Decision

The parser buffer is named after the file's real extension, never after its language

## Why

Measured: JSX is legal in `.js` and the TypeScript grammar reads `<div` as a type assertion, so 727 of react/react's 2,296 files and 3,924 of next.js's 21,358 were counted from a recovered tree, with nothing reporting it.
Always asking for the JSX grammar is not the fix: `<string>x` is legal in `.ts` and a syntax error in `.tsx`
