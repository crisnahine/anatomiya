# C71

Section: C. The counted-claim model

## Decision

Four language and row pairs the C51 bar refused are asked anyway, by the owner's decision of 2026-10-28 that every language gets the same rows where its grammar can spell the question: `caught_error_used` for Python, C# and Kotlin, and `class_inherits` for Kotlin. A fifth, `logger_over_print` for Go, clears the bar once only a call that writes a line counts

## Why

The bar keeps a row out of a language when its measured repositories agree, since a claim nobody breaks tells a session nothing it would not do anyway.
That is a cost of tokens, not of correctness: a row whose ratio clears 0.90 in an area is stated there, and the stated line is true.
The owner weighed that against one rule for every language and chose the rule, for these pairs, the ones the bar refused on `class_inherits` (C69), `logger_over_print` (C70) and the languages `caught_error_used` gained (C51).
The rows refused for another reason stay refused: a filename (H52), an import's names (C52) and a function's name (C54), each fixed for these languages by a compiler, a linter on by default or the language's own style guide, so a session cannot break them.
Through the shipped rows, on the clones `2026-10-28-tree-rows.md` lists, `caught_error_used` reads Python 1.0000 in all three (django 302, flask 18, fastapi 23 sites), C# 1.0000 in all three (jellyfin 434, serilog 18, Newtonsoft.Json 69), and Kotlin 0.7960, 0.8235, 0.7059 (okhttp 199 of 250, ktor 434 of 527, kotlinx.serialization 24 of 34), a spread of 0.1176.
`class_inherits` reads Kotlin 0.5310, 0.4206, 0.3846 (okhttp 77 of 145, ktor 270 of 642, kotlinx.serialization 95 of 247), a spread of 0.1464, object and companion object declarations counted.
`logger_over_print` reads Go 0.8223, 1.0000, 0.8920 (caddy 236 of 287, gin 5 of 5, hugo 190 of 213), a spread of 0.1777.
C70's first Go read, all over 0.90, counted every call into a logger package: `slog.String`, `zap.Error(err)` and `log.SetFlags` write nothing, and caddy alone holds 122 `zap.Error(err)`.
Jellyfin's 434 was checked by a separate regex over its `.cs` files: 423 named handlers, every one reading its name or throwing.
C51 read jellyfin 0.9321 and Newtonsoft.Json 0.8961 on 2026-10-07 by a script that is not this row; the heads have moved since.

What this gives up: in Python and C# the row is a line the map spends where every handler already uses its error; a Go logger reached through a name the rule does not know, `sugar.Infow`, reads as nothing.
A Kotlin `"$e"` is read off the template text: tree-sitter-kotlin 1.1.0, its latest release, parses `"${e}"` into an interpolation but `"$e"` into plain text, an open upstream bug (https://github.com/tree-sitter-grammars/tree-sitter-kotlin/issues/9).
An anonymous C# object, `new { e = 2 }`, holds its member and its value side by side with no field to tell them apart, so a member named as the error reads as a read.
