# D11

Section: D. Gates

## Decision

A clone holding only a window of history cannot lower the author bar. The bar holds at `GATES.authorEvidence`, and both the overview and the terminal say the history was truncated

## Why

`git log` succeeds on a shallow clone and answers truthfully about a window nobody chose, and nothing above it asked whether the window was the whole history.
Measured on one repository at three depths, same HEAD, same corpus, same 2,199 slots: the full clone states 465 claims with 20 held by the author gate and 13 authors at most; at `--depth=503` it states 383 with 105 held, losing 82 conventions and gaining none; at `--depth=1`, which is what `actions/checkout` does by default, every one of the 2,199 slots reads one author, the bar derived from that window collapses to one, the gate fires on nothing, and 484 claims state, 19 more than the honest 465 and every one of them on the single-author evidence D4 exists to refuse.
The map then printed `1 author (the repository's only)` 644 times and told the reader the repository has one author, over a repository with fifteen.
Nothing on any surface said the clone was shallow: 103 of 127 area files differ silently between the full and the truncated build.
Refusing to scan is the wrong answer, since CI checkouts are shallow by default and a map is still worth having there; what a depth-1 checkout may state is what one commit can evidence, which is nothing, so every claim prints as a count.
`historyRead` stays true, because a shallow history was read and conflating the two would print the wrong reason on every slot
