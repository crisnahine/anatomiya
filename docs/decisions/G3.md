# G3

Section: G. Scope

## Decision

Rename entries whose glossary name would misread as a verdict: `boundary_input_validation` not "Postel's Law", quarantine-rate not "Flaky test"

## Why

A rendered `1.0` next to "Postel's Law" reads as a philosophical endorsement rather than a measurement
