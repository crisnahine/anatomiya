# C8

Section: C. The counted-claim model

## Decision

A dimension whose claim belongs to a framework carries `framework`, and is offered only where the corpus shows that framework

## Why

Measured: `zone_aware_time` has no counter-claim, so off-Rails it can only ever print zero, and did: 123 sites on Homebrew, 197 on puppet, 97 on fastlane, 96 on chef, plus a NIT delivered onto a plain-Ruby branch.
The signal is `app/models/`, `db/migrate/` or `config/application.rb` in the corpus, which separates 14 Rails from 5 plain across 19 repositories; read from the corpus, because rubocop's only `app/models` files are three fixtures the corpus drops.
Only the two dimensions that cannot see their own context are marked.
The check asked its own question and never asked which frameworks are here, so the measured NIT on a plain-Ruby branch outlived the fix on the scan

## Status

**done** `walk.mjs` (`dimensionsFor`) `dimensions.mjs` (the framework field check) `corpus.mjs` `check.mjs`.
The registry no longer refuses a framework row on a language the oxc worker parses: the reducer and the check are the arbiters, both selecting through `dimensionsFor` with the frameworks the corpus shows, so the worker's unfiltered hits are read by no slot and no finding.
`adoptedCapabilities` is the one reader that takes those hits without selecting, and it is framework-blind, so a row may not carry `framework` and `capability` at once: otherwise off-framework hits would vote three files' worth of adoption for the capability rows the whole repository is then offered
