# B57

Section: B. Engine

## Decision

Where one of the two extensions a root prints is a component's, the other gets a namesake count of its own beside the first, whichever of the two is first, and the two are never summed

## Why

The clause for the smaller of the two populations prints from 3 files up, the floor H38 reads a directory at, or where a test credits at least one of its files, and the larger always prints.
A test's owner is looked for twice, among the sources of its own language and among the components of its family.
The namesake count runs over one extension, the first the line prints that this tool reads (H45), so element-plus `packages/components` printed `85 of 745` over its `.ts` files with its 164 components in no count, and kit `packages/kit` counted none of its `.svelte` files.
The count is a second clause on the same line.
Counting the component extension in place of the first would drop the larger count, since components are the smaller extension in both roots (777 `.ts` against 164 `.vue`, 1,064 `.js` against 923 `.svelte`).
A line of its own would take one of seven root lines. element-plus prints `85 of 745 .ts files have a namesake test; 81 of 164 .vue files have a namesake test` on its root line and its tests line, and `85 of 745` is unchanged.
Of the 81, 78 are imported by name by the test credited and the other 3 sit in its package.
Taken only for a component extension printed second, the count leaves out the modules of a root that holds more components than modules: vitepress `src/client/theme-default` reads `66 .vue, 20 .ts and 28 other; 1 of 66 has a namesake test`, with its 20 modules and the directory their 6 tests sit in on no line.
Taken for either order it reads `1 of 66 .vue files has a namesake test; 6 of 20 .ts files have a namesake test under __tests__/unit/client/theme-default`.
Printed for every second population, an area's kinds line carries both clauses on 41 of element-plus's 113 area files, 38 of shadcn-svelte's 88, 2 of vitepress's 27 and 1 of kit's 110, and 29 of shadcn-svelte's 38 read `0 of 1` for a lone file, 28 of them `0 of 1 .ts file have a namesake test` for an `index.ts`.
With the smaller population printed from 3 files up the four read 22, 7, 0 and 1 and 52 clauses go, 37 of them reading 0 and 15 a true count with a credit in it, all 15 on element-plus, `1 of 2 .vue files has a namesake test` or the like over 17 credited components.
Printed from 3 files up or where one file is credited the four read 37, 7, 0 and 1: the 37 clauses that go all read 0, on 37 of the 338 area files, none of which gains or loses a line, and shadcn-svelte prints no `0 of 1`.
The root line and the tests line of each are the same either way, vitepress `src/client/theme-default` printing both over its 66 `.vue` and 20 `.ts`.
A line left with one clause prints it bare, as a root with one count does.
A test credits a component by its stem, under the rule a `.tsx` file gets, or by importing it under its full name: `./Foo.vue` covers `Foo.vue`, and a bare `./Foo` never resolves to a component, since a bundler needs the extension spelled and a stem answering both `Foo.ts` and `Foo.vue` is the ambiguity the resolver exists to refuse.
One owner over the whole family takes `button.test.tsx` off `button.ts` for the `button.vue` it imports, and element-plus reads 38 of 745 `.ts` files in place of 85, so the owner is asked once per group (H27): a test owned by `select.ts` still answers `select.vue`.
Among components, one under the test's own directory, less the test tree words that directory ends in, is preferred to any mirror.
With no owner among components three area lines are false: element-plus `docs/examples` reads `1 of 239 has a namesake test` for `docs/examples/autocomplete/autocomplete.vue` against a test that imports `../src/autocomplete.vue` in its own package, and shadcn-svelte reads `1 of 104` and `1 of 182` in two docs areas for `message-scroller.test.ts`, which imports no component by that name and sits one directory above the one it covers.
With it all three read 0, and across vitepress, element-plus, kit and shadcn-svelte the only other credits to move are `select.vue` and `tree.vue`, each imported by the test it gains.
A component with no script still owes a test.
No component is a test file, whatever it is named, wherever it sits and whatever its script holds, since no runner collects a `.vue` or `.svelte` file: vitepress keeps the 5 theme components of its e2e site under `__tests__`, and with the `__tests__` directory rule applied to them its overview reads `__tests__: 78 test files and 60 other` where it holds 73 (20 of which hold no case and are a test file by the directory's name alone: the fixture and e2e sites' configuration, setup files and data loaders), and the area of four of them reads `kinds: 4 .vue; 4 test files`.
A component under a test tree of its family, a `__tests__` directory or a package's own `test`, is a fixture of the tests there and is in neither number of the namesake count, as a file found empty is, with no clause of its own (`isProducer`, H21).
Of the four repositories vitepress holds 5 under `__tests__`, in a root that is asked no namesake question, and kit keeps the components of its test applications under `packages/kit/test`, 919 of the 922 `.svelte` files of that root that hold something, so its line reads `64 of 224 .js files have a namesake test under packages/kit; 0 of 3 .svelte files have a namesake test`

The second count is taken only over an extension the line already printed, and `otherCompanions` is absent on every root with no component among its two printed extensions, so no other repository's record moves: errbit, typeorm and mastodon, which hold no component, write the same map files and the same record with and without it.
What this gives up: a component whose stem no other component carries has no owner to lose to, so a test elsewhere of that stem credits it on path shape, as `Foo.tsx` with `Foo.test.ts` already is; a `.ts` test of two `.tsx` files of one stem is asked among `.ts` sources alone; the component that owns `message-scroller.test.ts` is itself uncredited, the test sitting one directory above it and importing nothing by its name; a count of 0 over one or two files of the second kind, so two components nothing tests beside a directory of modules are on no namesake clause of the kinds line; where the two populations are one size the second printed is the one held to the floor; a root that keeps its test applications inside it counts few of the components its extension line shows, kit printing `0 of 3 .svelte files` beside `923 .svelte`; `Counter.svelte.test.ts` does not credit `Counter.svelte`, its stem being `Counter.svelte`; and the precedent reason and the principles still read the first count alone
