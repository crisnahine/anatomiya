# A22

Section: A. Delivery

## Decision

The overview's "Not covered" section names a language this tool has no dimension for, counted over the whole corpus rather than over what the roster printed

## Why

`plugins/anatomiya/lib/langs.mjs` declares three: js, jsx, ruby.
Everything else failed `isSource()` and joined the same undifferentiated bucket as images and markdown, and nothing in the rendered prose ever said so.
Measured: appsmith's `app/server` is 2,374 files, 2,077 of them `.java` with a real JUnit suite, reduced in the whole map to one raw extension count on a root line; next.js holds 1,016 `.rs` files across its own bundler and compiler, and the words Rust and cargo appeared in zero of its 501 generated files.
A reader had no way to learn that a third of a repository, or an entire embedded subsystem, was never read

## Status

**done** `render.mjs` (`OTHER_LANGUAGE_EXTS`, `unreadLanguageFiles`), `scan.mjs` (`corpus.otherExts`), `facts.mjs` (schema 13), pinned by `test/render.test.mjs`.
The count is taken from the corpus's own tally over every non-source file rather than summed back off the roster: a root prints its top two extensions and folds the rest away, which held 781 of next.js's 1,016 Rust files, and a row about what could not be read is the last place to state a number it cannot stand behind.
The list of other languages is short and closed rather than exhaustive, so a missing entry is silence about a language rather than a wrong name for one, and a record written before schema 13 falls back to the roster and undercounts the same way, which is still better than the silence it replaced.
The registry declares twelve languages (B21).
The list holds 39 extensions: 15 of the 23 that release 0.13.4 listed, whose other 8 are the ones a tree-sitter grammar reads (`.py`, `.php`, `.go`, `.java`, `.cs`, `.rs`, `.kt`, `.kts`), and 24 added after a Rails fixture printed nothing about its twelve unread `.erb` and `.css` files: templates (`.erb`, `.haml`, `.slim`, `.html`, `.htm`, `.astro`), stylesheets (`.css`, `.scss`, `.sass`, `.less`), `.sh`, `.bash` and `.sql`, and eleven more languages.
Measured on three corpus repositories against the list without the 24: errbit prints `82 files hold a language this map does not read (72 .erb, 5 .css, 5 .html)` where it printed no such line, mastodon prints the line with six names (`310 .haml, 91 .scss, 47 .erb, 10 .css, 8 .sql, 3 .html`, 469 files) where it printed none, and webpack's goes from `1 file ... (1 .cc)` to `43 files ... (31 .html, 11 .css, 1 .cc)`.
The line is not capped: six names at 107 characters is the longest measured, shorter than lines the overview already prints.
On errbit and mastodon the overview already sat at its forty lines, so the roster pays for the line: it folds one root line into its `and N more directories` line.
What the folded root line gives up is where its tests live (mastodon lost `under spec/serializers`).
The areas under that root still print their own namesake counts.
