# C39

Section: C. The counted-claim model

## Decision

The witness gate drives three more tables through their rows: `RUBY_ERROR` and the `Errno::` prefix through `class_base` as members that produce no site, `FRAMEWORK` through `migration_schema_only` as members whose site conforms, and `EFFECT_HOOKS` through `absent_is_null` as members whose callback is not a site; each such row carries a control that has to answer the other way

## Why

The tables were checked by a hand list against the set lookup, not by driving members through the predicate, and a plain witness row demands a hit per member where these produce none.
The gate has three expectations now, present, absent and conforming, and the tables are exported so the grew-or-shrank comparison covers them too
