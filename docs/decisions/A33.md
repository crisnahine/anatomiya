# A33

Section: A. Delivery

## Decision

An area's kinds line is taken off the line bound before the body divides what is left, rather than entered into the body as a block that competes with claim rows

## Why

Left to compete it lost, and it lost in exactly the directories where it is worth the most.
Measured on a 2,489-file front end at 0.2.9: five area files carried no kinds line at all, and they were the five largest, `src/components` at 149 files, `src/queries` at 141, `src/pages` at 75, `src/components/TaskList/tasks` at 75 and `src/pages/admin` at 41.
All 122 smaller areas kept theirs, including areas of one file.
The boundary was the budget and it was exact: the most claims hidden by an area that kept the line was 15, the fewest hidden by an area that lost it was 17, and no area sat between.
The data was there in every case, `corpus.truncated` was false, and the line was computed and then dropped at render.
What it costs a reader is the overview's own headline directive: "match sibling test shape, skip tests where siblings have none" cannot be followed in a directory whose file never said what the siblings do.
It comes out of the floor as well as out of the bound, so `head + kinds + body` totals exactly what `head + body` totalled before and no file grew by this; six body lines still hold one directive block, an exception and the notice
