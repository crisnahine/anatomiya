# A110

Section: A. Delivery

## Decision

A co-change block renders after its area's directives, keyed null in `areaBlocks`, and the directives settle first with the blocks taken out. Blocks take only the lines that leaves, with the "and N more" line owed; where not even that line fits, they go unsaid and the check is told. A claim whose producers fall in no one area, or in an area whose globs reach no producer at the tip, is printed nowhere and checked at FIX

## Why

`settle` reserves a name line per hidden stated key, and a keyed block would move which directives an older check believes printed; settled with the blocks in the list, a block could push a directive out at an exact-fit height.
Settling the directives first keeps every directive's printed or dropped fate the one it has with no block at all, swept at 0 to 45 directives with blocks present.
Against 0.14.0 on the 60 corpus repositories, blocks land in 13 area files of 7 repositories and no directive line moves in any of them; in one supabase area four gated count lines give way to its two blocks and fold into `and 4 more not shown here, all of them counts`, the order the budget drops in.
A block in an area file whose globs never load for the producer's kind spends that file's budget on readers it never reaches: supabase's two `.mdx` claims printed in area files for `.ts` and `.tsx`.
The overview is byte-stable and cannot carry per-pair lines
