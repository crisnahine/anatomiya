# B64

Section: B. Engine

## Decision

The engine parses a `.cs` file its grammar rejects at most twice more, and takes a retry only where its tree is clean: first with a line break appended where the file ends without one, then with every directive line and every branch of each `#if` but the first blanked in place. A file that lost a branch holding anything is marked `oneBranch`, counted on a line of its own, and named by `check`

## Why

The grammar reads a directive around whole statements and members only, and rejects a last line that is a `#pragma`, `#endregion` or `#nullable` with no line break.
Read as written, 18 of serilog's 216 files and 66 of Newtonsoft.Json's 951 are rejected, 27.7% and 25.9% of their lines.
The appended line break reads 23 of the 66 and drops nothing.
The blanking reads 17 of serilog's 18 and the other 43, and 7 and 31 of those are marked `oneBranch`. jellyfin is unchanged at 1 of 2,247.
Blanked means spaces with line breaks kept, so no offset or line moves, and the text the tree was read from rides on the record so a row in the scan and in the check reads the same string.
Given up: what the other branches of 38 files hold is counted nowhere, and a file is parsed up to three times.
A later attempt starts only while the time spent on the file plus one more parse as long as the first stays inside 4 seconds (`RETRY_BUDGET_MS`), under the pool's 5-second clock (B66), so a file too large for that is left rejected with a retry untried
