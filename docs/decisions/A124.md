# A124

Section: A. Delivery

## Decision

Where the scan wrote a code index for the checkout the session sits in, or for its main checkout where it is a linked worktree, the reuse check's reason (A91, A123) asks the subagent to run `find`, by the absolute path of the bin that ran the hook, with two or three words with no quote mark for what each added function does, at most three calls, and to grep only for code making the same calls. `find` prints six hits. Where there is no index, or the path to the bin cannot be quoted safely, the reason is A123's word for word

## Why

The index lists a repository's functions with their parameters, doc sentence and importers, which a grep over shared modules finds a file at a time.
A first build appended a sentence naming `find` after the measured wording, and the subagent ran `find` and every grep as well; a second replaced the grep sentence.
Measured with A125 in place (`docs/measurements/2026-10-28-code-index.md`), on four reuse tasks with Haiku 5.5 at 10 sessions per arm, 37 of 40 sessions called the helper against 0.19.0's 65 of 80, at a mean session cost of $0.0153 against $0.0164: the result held at about its cost.
On the build that ships, at 20 sessions per arm on the same four tasks, 67 of 80 called the helper against 0.19.0's 65 of 80, at $0.0157 a session against $0.0164.
On the build after the PR review, the five per-language tasks of 0.19.0 not run before (Python, PHP, Java, C#, Kotlin), 10 sessions per arm: 50 of 50 with anatomiya called the helper, against 46 of 50 without.
Ten sessions per arm cannot separate those from chance, and Opus 5.5 was not run, so no gain in calls is claimed.
That build printed twelve hits and asked for one call per idea, and its subagent spent more tokens than the same check with no index: the index was paid for, not saved by.
Three builds were then run on the same Claude Code, 2.1.312, on mastodon's link policy and eslint, 10 sessions per arm (`docs/measurements/2026-10-28-code-index.md`): no index in the reason, 16 of 20 at $0.0171 a session; twelve hits, 17 of 20 at $0.0177; six hits and three calls, 18 of 20 at $0.0164.
On the last, the subagent's median fresh input was 24% and 45% lower than with no index, its output 34% and 31% lower, and it made 6 and 6.5 tool calls against 8 and 10 (`docs/measurements/data/2026-10-28-reuse-search-tokens.json`).
That is the build that ships.
The words go in one single-quoted argument, since the Bash tool runs the user's shell and zsh reads `local_url?` as a pattern and refuses it with "no matches found"; `find` splits one argument into its words as it splits several.
In a linked worktree the index is the main checkout's, so a hit can name a line on another branch, and the subagent reads the file before it calls anything.
The path is the running bin's own, so the copy that answers is the build that wrote the index; one holding a character a shell reads inside double quotes (`$`, a backtick, a quote) falls back to the grep wording.
