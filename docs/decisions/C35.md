# C35

Section: C. The counted-claim model

## Decision

A declared type name that reads as a prefix and as an acronym alike - two capitals, two capitals and a digit, or three or more leading ones - votes for neither prefix nor none, and is a site of neither prefix row

## Why

`prefixClass` wanted a lowercase third character, so `IQRScanner` fell through to `none` and was charged with violating the `I` prefix it already carries.
The only way to comply with a stated `I` claim was to write `IIQRScanner`.
Measured on a 2,489-file front end: `src/components` recorded `interface_prefix` at 157 of 164 where the true figure is 157 of 159, five of the seven charged sites carrying the prefix, and it named `QRScanner/index.tsx` and `QRScanner/types.ts` as the area's exceptions when neither holds a violation, so anything added to those two files was waved through as already excepted.
In an area stating the row at N of N the same shape reaches MUST-FIX: `IApiResponse` conforms and `IAPIResponse` is a finding, the only difference being that the acronym is capitalised.
The row is recorded in 116 of that repository's 127 areas and stated at N of N in 87, so this is the normal case.
The design comment defended declining to credit `IOStream` with an `I`, which is right, and never noticed the two shapes are the same shape to this regex.
What it costs is a denominator: an area near the evidence floor that loses two names can stop stating, and under a stated `I` claim `ABCFoo` now passes rather than being charged.
That is the trade C33 already takes, and it is the right direction here because the unreadable shape is the realistic conforming one rather than the realistic violating one, which is what separates this from C21's `classifyWord` narrowing.
The same shape opens every mixed-case acronym: `OAuthToken` voted `O`, `ETag` `E` and `IDs` `I`, so only `I`, `T` and `E` vote as a prefix, and not where the name opens on a known acronym ending with its word (`IDs`, `IPv4`, `ETag`); any other capital-capital-lower opening votes for neither.
Two capitals and a digit is the two-capital case over again: `IV8Profile` is `I` on `V8Profile` and `ID3Tag` is the tag of an `ID3`, and no pattern separates them.
Read as carrying no prefix, `IV8Profile` broke a stated `I` claim that no spelling could keep.
On the 35 corpus repositories 7 such names leave the count in 3 areas, 6 in two areas of vscode (`10 of 13` to `10 of 11`, `11 of 21` to `11 of 17`) and 1 in angular (`29 of 43` to `28 of 42`), and no stated claim moves
