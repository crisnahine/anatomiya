# D13

Section: D. Gates

## Decision

A line names the author count of the side it prints, stated or not, and every tally of author-gated claims counts the printed side's gate

## Why

`statedSide` shows an unstated slot on its counter side where the counter holds a supportable majority, with the counter's gate, and D4 already counts authors per side, but the record carried only the claim side's count.
So 40 sites by one author printed `authors 0 of 2`: 21 such lines on a clone of the client corpus repository, and the terminal said 1 claim was held on the author gate where the map printed 3
