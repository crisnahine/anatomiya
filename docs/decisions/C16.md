# C16

Section: C. The counted-claim model

## Decision

A framework is a profile in `plugins/anatomiya/lib/frameworks.mjs`, and `frameworksIn` folds over the profiles as a set

## Why

The fold returned a `Set` and every consumer treated it as one, but the body broke out of the loop after the first hit behind a `lang === "ruby"` gate, so a corpus holding two frameworks could only ever be one of them, and the check's probe carried its own copy of the gate as a language literal.
One profile carries its name, its language and its measured signal; the fold asks every profile until it answers, the same shape `capabilitiesIn` already has; the check asks `couldSignal` instead of naming Ruby; and the closed name set the `framework` field is asserted against is the profiles', so the two cannot drift.
Deliberately not built until a second framework exists: a full profile owning its rows, its obligations and its runtime packages, because one adapter means a hypothetical seam and the nine Rails obligations are offered by companion-suffix use, not by framework, which this row does not change
