## Decision

Whether a path is corpus is asked of one function, `isCorpusPath`, over the rule list `classify` refuses by, and the reads that need the file open stay with `collect`

## Why

The three path predicates were composed by hand at three call sites, and a fourth, wrong, composition in the harness's probe missed every `.mjs` repository.
One spelling over a private `pathDrop` naming the refusing rule keeps the four dropped counts `collect` reports (A21, G10) and lets the check, the notice and the probe ask one question.
The generated-file head read and the realpath check are not path-only: a diff path at a base sha need not exist on disk, and the notice runs inside the pre-write hook where a per-path open was refused on cost (A44)
