# C41

Section: C. The counted-claim model

## Decision

`service_result_shape` counts a bang call that raises on failure as raising unless a rescue guards it, and its sentence says what is measured: entry points do not raise, directly or through a bang call

## Why

36 services failing through an unrescued `update!` stated 'return their failure instead of raising' at 36 of 36, and `check` gave a FIX to a sibling that failed the same way.
On empire-flippers/api 38 service areas stated it; `app/services/models/tasks/sellers` read 52 of 55 with most conforming sites raising through `update!` or `run!`, and entry points with no failure path at all were credited with returning one.
A closed table, because `merge!` and `strip!` are bangs that fail nothing

## Status

**done** `dimensions-ruby.mjs` (`RAISING_BANG`, `caught`), pinned by `test/ruby.test.mjs` and `test/applicability.test.mjs`.
A bang call under any rescue counts as caught, whatever class the rescue names, and a non-bang raiser such as `find` is not counted.
The row states no inverse: most entry points that do not raise have no failure to raise, and 'raise on failure' stated over 36 `save!` services gave a FIX to a pure `Slugify.call`.
A map written with that inverse stated is enforced on neither side by `check` (`introduced.mjs`)
