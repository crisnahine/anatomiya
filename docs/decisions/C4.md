# C4

Section: C. The counted-claim model

## Decision

Applicability floor: the stricter of two, with the share capped at three roots, `applicability >= max(R, min(ceil(0.25 * F), 3R))` where `R = ceil(sqrt(F))` and `F` is the files the dimension can speak about

## Why

v3 promised this floor and gave it no number, no gate, no storage, no test.
Each floor alone is wrong: the root asks for more than a quarter below sixteen files, where a quarter of a small directory is one or two files, and the share is what holds above it.
Measured: the root alone asked 11 files of 120, and a 120-file area where 11 files used `?.` and 109 read absent values without it stated the claim over all 120

## Status

**done** `reduce.mjs` (`GATES.minApplicabilityShare`, gate name `applicability`).
`F` is the files the dimension could speak about, and a file nothing was read from is not one of them: `applicability` counts files that produced a site, so a denominator over every file of the language divided one population by another.
Measured over a 35-repository corpus, on the repositories that hold syntax this tool does not take: react is written in Flow, 287 of its 2,277 files are rejected, 55 of its 122 areas hold at least one and one area holds nothing else, and its stated claims go from 36 to 65 once the denominator counts only what was examined.
Webpack 80 to 92, next.js 162 to 172.
None of those claims was suppressed because a predicate was narrow.
An obligation is counted the same way, since `applyPairings` skips a record that is not ok and an unread producer answers nothing.
The count is taken off the records handed in rather than off the area's file list, which is what makes `applicability <= langFileCount` hold in both passes: the pinned pass reduces records keyed by the pinned paths against an area holding today's, so reading `area.files` dropped every file renamed since the pin, and twelve renames with no content change took a slot from suppressed to stated.
That is the laundering the pinned population exists to refuse, arriving through the denominator The share is capped because it was measured at 120 files and never tested at 1,531: it grows linearly with the area while the risk it guards does not, so on a single 1,531-file `db/migrate` it asked for 383 files and made any construct rarer than a quarter of the directory unstateable however perfect.
`table_primary_key_declared` there is 152 of 154, ratio 0.987, bound 0.954, and it is the failure the cap was written for.
It was not the only one: the uncapped floor closed two slots in that map, the other being `zone_aware_time` at 63 of 63 over 22 of 117 files in one test tree, which is a small area rather than a large one and is the case the root half of the floor is for.
Three roots is the smallest whole factor that leaves the measured band alone: the first area size where the cap changes the answer is 157, above every area the share was measured on, and at two roots it is 73 and `floor(120)` falls from 30 to 22, which moves the answer on the very area this row was written from.
Still monotone in the area size, checked to 200,000.
The floor is one exported function, `applicabilityFloor`, because two tests re-implemented it locally and would have stayed green through a broken change.
`scripts/audit-applicability.mjs`'s own `NARROW = 0.25` is a ranking threshold for which rows are worth opening, not this gate, and the two must not be unified.
